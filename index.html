FAQ

Who made ScotPapers?

Hi, I'm QWK and I made ScotPapers.
I'm a Scottish student in S6 looking to study Computing Science at university.

Do you make money from this?

No, sadly not

Is this vibe coded / Do you use AI?

No, everything has been hand coded by me,
and all the moderation/sorting is done manually by humans

Where do you get these papers from?

All over the internet.

Scotpapers is an archival website, which means that any past paper
you can find here, you can find somewhere else;
although that place may be the tenth page of Google.

I've made this as an easy to access archive for every subject from 2015 I can find online.

Can you add a subject?

If you have the files or know where to get them, then 100% yes!
Email me them at: qwk.contact@gmail.com

If you don't have any files, I can try my best but often older papers are impossible to find,
especially the audio files for modern languages

How can I get in contact?

Leave an inquiry with your email/discord here.
Me/my mods will go through these requests occasionally, so please have patience and please don't fill up the inquiry board with bogus reviews!
